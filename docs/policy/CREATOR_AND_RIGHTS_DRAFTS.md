## Copyright & Attribution Notice

Lolixco curates video edits through official YouTube embeds and, where an appropriate
basis for use has been established, hosts selected files. Creators retain their
rights in their contributions. Music, recordings, film, television, anime, games,
characters and other underlying material may belong to their respective owners.
A creator credit identifies the credited contribution; it does not imply that the
creator or Lolixco owns every underlying element or that a rights holder endorses
Lolixco. No ownership of third-party works is claimed by Lolixco.

Attribution, a disclaimer, noncommercial intent, online availability and willingness
to remove a work do not themselves grant permission to redistribute it. YouTube's
availability or embedding arrangements do not establish independent hosting rights.

## Creator Credit Policy

Show the verified editor/channel name beside content in cards and players, retaining
source title/URL and required license attribution where the permission requires it.
Distinguish the editor, soundtrack contributors and underlying rights holders.
Never invent an editor name, source URL or license. Mark unresolved attribution in
the private release tracker and resolve required credits before release. A generic
"Lolixco" source label describes the hosting platform, not authorship.

For official YouTube embeds, preserve channel attribution and canonical source
link. For hosted edits, verify the editor's identity and each applicable license's
required title, author, source, license URL/version, modification notice and other
conditions. Do not imply a YouTube creator authorized independent hosting merely
because they uploaded an edit. Applicable published licenses and direct grants
are acceptable evidence; bespoke certificates are not universally required.

Report a mistaken credit using [rights@golixco.com](mailto:rights@golixco.com), identifying the content
URL, proposed correction and supporting source. We review corrections and preserve
the original record and decision history. A credit correction does not itself settle
hosting permission.

## Rights-holder contact and removal workflow

Contact [rights@golixco.com](mailto:rights@golixco.com) with:

- The Lolixco content URL or ID and relevant timestamps.
- Your name, role and reliable reply contact; agent authority where applicable.
- The work or contribution you claim rights in, and the nature of those rights.
- Source links, license terms or other supporting information and requested action.
- Whether the request concerns removal/restriction, attribution or both.

Provide necessary information only; do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive
records. We assess each report promptly, seek clarification when needed, preserve
relevant evidence, and restrict or remove affected material where appropriate.
We will not treat an unverified allegation as an automatic legal determination.
For YouTube embeds, Lolixco can remove or restrict its listing; the source remains
under the source platform's controls. For hosted files, restricting the catalog
alone is insufficient: origin delivery and cached copies must be addressed.
Already downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

## Terms of Use / Content Policy

Lolixco provides curated playback, not a general grant to download, copy, remix or
redistribute third-party content. Official YouTube embeds remain subject to their
platform's controls and applicable terms. Hosted files have an independently
reviewed hosting basis; being playable does not grant viewers additional rights.
Users must respect applicable rights and conditions, and must not bypass access
controls. Availability and attribution can change following review.

Submissions must identify the editor and disclose the source and basis for using
video, music/recordings and the finished edit. Ownership, an applicable published
license, direct authorization, or an applicable exception supported by an appropriate
assessment may establish a basis. Missing evidence is investigated rather than
represented as clearance or proof of infringement. Restricted or conflicting
permissions must be resolved before the affected use. Replacement of approved
content requires an owner decision. This policy does not authorize a use prohibited
by law or license.
